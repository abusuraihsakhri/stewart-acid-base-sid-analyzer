Equations

SIDa = Na + K + 2·iCa + 2·iMg − Cl − lactate
HCO₃ = 0.0301 · PaCO₂ · 10^(pH − 6.1)
Alb⁻ = albumin · (0.123·pH − 0.631)
Pi⁻ = phosphate · (0.309·pH − 0.469)
SIDe = HCO₃ + Alb⁻ + Pi⁻
SIG = SIDa − SIDe

SIDa and SIG are in mEq/L. Equations: Stewart–Figge approximation. Divalent cation inputs in mmol/L are explicitly multiplied by 2.
